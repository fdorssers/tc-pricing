## Scope

`index.html`, the whole price site. Mode: Operate. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. The user wants the generic AI-dashboard look (dark green-black, rounded stat cards, big numbers) replaced; structure may change, but every product question must still be answered.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Variegation is what a TC buyer pays for, so the price table reads like a horticultural colour chart: every plant carries its colour code. It refuses the category default of a dark dashboard with rounded stat cards and giant percentages.

OWN-WORLD: Matte grey card stock (charcoal card in dark mode). Near-black ink, hairline grey rules, square 2–4px corners, no shadows. Colour is spent on swatches only: Albo cream-white, Aurea yellow, Mint pale green, Pink, Lava red-orange, leaf green. Combined types and Tricolor are multi-cell strips, and unspecified variegation is a hatched cell. Sellers are dark inks. Up and down stay red and green, text-sized only. Type is Archivo: names in regular weight, small caps for genus and swatch codes, tabular figures.

STORY: The visitor filters by variegation with a swatch chip, scans one dense row per plant (swatch strip, name, seller prices, spread), sees the cheapest price underlined in place, and opens a plant's history to see its price over time.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Header: title and the list date.
- Filter bar: search, genus, seller, and the swatch-chip fan with counts.
- Seller strip: one flat three-cell row with the basket change and cheapest share.
- Table: swatch strip, plant (genus code above the name), one column per seller, then Spread.
- Rows are about 40px; roughly twelve fit on a laptop screen.
